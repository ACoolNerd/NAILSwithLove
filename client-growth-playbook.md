## 5. Services and common nail types

- **Regular polish:** easy removal, shorter wear; good for clients who change often.
- **Semipermanent/gel polish:** cured under lamp; longer shine; removal must be gentle.
- **Rubber base / BIAB / builder overlay:** reinforces the natural nail; clarify that brands and techniques differ.
- **Soft gel tips / Gel-X style:** full-cover extension; light feel and consistent shape.
- **Acrylic:** liquid-and-powder enhancement; strong and sculptable; requires maintenance.
- **Polygel/acrygel:** hybrid-feel sculpting product; availability depends on technician training.
- **Press-ons:** reusable or temporary custom sets; confirm sizing and adhesive method.

Common shapes: round, oval, squoval, almond, coffin/ballerina, square, stiletto. Common looks: French, baby boomer/ombré, nude, chrome, cat-eye, aura, glazed, micro-French, floral, encapsulated glitter, 3D accents. Price detailed work by complexity and time, not by vague labels.

## 7. Policies to publish

- Deposit amount, when it becomes non-refundable, and how rescheduling works.
- A reasonable notice window (recommend 12–24 hours).
- Late-arrival rule (for example, after 15 minutes the service may be shortened or moved).
- Travel fee and exact coverage area.
- Removal, repair, length, and art are priced separately unless stated.
- A brief workmanship window (for example, contact within 48 hours with photos); exclude impact, picking, chemicals, and misuse.
- Hygiene/health refusal policy and patch/sensitivity considerations where relevant.
- Photo consent must be optional and documented.

## 8. Funnel and numbers

Track weekly: inquiries, qualified leads, deposits, completed bookings, average order value, rebooking, referrals, no-shows, and ad spend.

Formulas: inquiry-to-deposit = deposits ÷ inquiries; show rate = completed ÷ deposits; cost per booking = ad spend ÷ deposits; revenue per appointment = service + add-ons − discounts. Do not scale ads until the business knows product cost, travel cost, and effective hourly earnings.
