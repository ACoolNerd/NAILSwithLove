## Bank presentation caution

The financial section is an illustrative planning model, not historical performance. It assumes:

- COP $95,000 average ticket
- 18% product cost
- COP $12,000 average transport per appointment
- 1.5% payment costs
- COP $550,000 monthly fixed marketing/operating expenses
- Indicative COP $8.5 million funding request

Replace assumptions with actual sales records, supplier quotations, transport costs, bank statements, and formal funding requirements before submitting a loan or grant application.
