## The opportunity

Women in Cali increasingly seek nail services that combine:
- Convenience and privacy (home or mobile)
- Consistent hygiene and biosecurity
- Transparent, appointment-based pricing
- Personalized designs and a trusted technician

Traditional walk-in salons lack appointment reliability, personalization, and mobile reach.

## Risk factors

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Demand lower than projected | Start lean; break-even requires only 23 appointments/month |
| Key-person dependency | Build brand + systems; train an assistant in year 2 |
| Property land-use denial | Pivot 100% to mobile until compliant space secured |
| Input cost increases | Review pricing quarterly; renegotiate with suppliers |
| Non-payment from clients | Deposit policy; payment verified before service |
